## Current Projects

My primary methodological areas of interest are Bayesian hierarchical modeling, dependent data (spatial statistics, time series, and functional data analysis), and adaptive clinical trials.  I am interested in many application areas. Currently, I am working on projects that fall into one or more of the following areas:

- Adaptive clinical trial design
- Medical statistics
- Modeling biodiversity
- Using historical and natural history data for early-phase clinical trials
- Developing valid (cross-)covariance models for Gaussian process models in various geometries
- Applied veterinary science
- Applied environmental, ecological, and climate data problems
