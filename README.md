## 👋 About Me

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Statistics at BYU. I also hold a curtesy appointment in the BYU School of Medicine. My research focuses on:

- Adaptive clinical trial design  
- Medical Statistics
- Bayesian hierarchical modeling  
- Spatial and spatiotemporal statistics  
- Functional data analysis  
- Time series  

I am particularly interested in applied problems that motivate methodological innovation. 

---

## 🔬 Research

My current work spans several areas, including:

- Dose escalation trials  
- Use of historical data in early-phase clinical trials  
- Biodiversity modeling  
- Gaussian process covariance modeling  
- Spatiotemporal and point process models  

See the full research page on my website for details.
